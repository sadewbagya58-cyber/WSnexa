# WSNexa Business Owner Loyalty Guide

## Getting Started
1. **Enable Program**: Go to `/dashboard/loyalty` and switch on your loyalty program.
2. **Choose Earning Model**: Select **Spend Based** (e.g. 1 point per 100 LKR spent), **Visit Based** (e.g. 10 points per visit), or **Combined**.
3. **Create Rewards**: Go to `/dashboard/loyalty/rewards` to add rewards like *"Free Coffee — 80 Points"* or *"LKR 500 Discount — 250 Points"*.
4. **Manage Customers**: Go to `/dashboard/loyalty/customers` to view customer points, visits, and adjust points for customer service recoveries.
